We've elevated the classic switcha with a touch of carbonation that makes the flavour pop and delivers a crisp, refreshing experience unlike any other.

Each can captures the perfect balance of tangy and sweet, with a bubbly twist that invigorates your taste buds and leaves you craving more. Whether you're relaxing on the beach or enjoying a sunny afternoon in PEI — this is your go-to drink for ultimate refreshment.

Carbonated for an extra layer of refreshment, this flavour strikes the perfect balance between sweet, tart, and zesty — with a bubbly twist that makes it truly unforgettable. Whether you're craving something adventurous or simply love a refreshing bold drink — this is the one.
